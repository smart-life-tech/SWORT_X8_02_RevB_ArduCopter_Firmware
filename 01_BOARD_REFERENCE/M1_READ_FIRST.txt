IMPORTANT STATUS NOTE
The findings register marks all Milestone 1 review items CLOSED, using explicit
closure bases (verified in the supplied connectivity, accepted/retained, or
outside the agreed hardware scope). CLOSED is a disposition status; it must not
be interpreted as evidence of physical prototype testing, firmware validation,
manufacturer approval, or a completed Milestone 2 manufacturing release.

The project database, PDF and netlist are supplied snapshots. They have been
copied into this package without editing their internal CAD contents during
packaging. Where an acceptance/limitation is identified in the disposition
register, that status is documented rather than presented as a new physical
modification to the board.
